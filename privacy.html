Privacy notice

Last updated November 23, 2026.

What these forms collect

We collect the information you submit, such as your name, email, optional phone number, volunteer interests, and message. Newsletter signup records your request to receive email updates.

How it is used

Submissions are stored in the website’s hosted database for HOOD to review and use to respond, coordinate volunteering, maintain its newsletter list, or follow up about membership. Newsletter signup does not apply for housing or reserve a place on a waiting list.

Volunteer signup through Tally

Volunteer signup links open our form hosted by Tally. Information entered there is submitted through Tally for HOOD to review and coordinate volunteering. The privacy terms of Tally also apply to use of that service.

Your choices

You can request correction or deletion of a submission, withdraw newsletter consent, or ask a privacy question through the contact form. Select “Other” and describe your request. Please do not submit sensitive identity, banking, or financial records.

Payments

The current website does not collect card details or process payments. Donation and membership payment setup is pending.
