The HOOD Movement

Homes are the start.
Community is the point.

People should be able to stay in the neighborhoods they helped build.

HOOD Community Land Trust brings together neighbors, property owners, builders, and supporters around a shared purpose: land held for community and homes that remain affordable for generations.

Through the HOOD Movement, we are building participation in that work—preserving existing housing, exploring new ways to create homes, and putting community stewardship at the center.

Our program priorities

Build opportunity.
Protect what matters.

Housing stability takes more than new construction. Our planned work supports the homes, people, and neighborhoods already here.

Foreclosure prevention

Help homeowners find housing counseling, understand available resources, and explore preservation options before a home is lost.

Preserve & renovate

Acquire and renovate rent-controlled and other at-risk rental properties, with a focus on resident stability and long-term stewardship.

Trust ownership

Hold land for community benefit and develop shared-equity pathways that balance household opportunity with lasting affordability.

Small-site housing

Explore ADUs, gentle infill, small-home communities, and overlooked sites that can become places to belong.

Resident leadership

Bring neighbors into the conversation and create ways for residents to help shape the future of their communities.

Long-term stewardship

Plan for maintenance, responsible reinvestment, and affordability protections that carry forward to the next generation.

A few helpful answers

Get to know HOOD.

Are there homes available right now?

HOOD is developing its organizational capacity, partnerships, and project pipeline. Housing availability, applications, and eligibility will be announced when programs are ready. Newsletter signup is not a housing application or waiting list.

Is HOOD a nonprofit?

Yes. HOOD Community Land Trust is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our federal tax ID is 38-4386684.

Can I bring a property or partnership opportunity?

Yes. Use our contact form to share a potential acquisition, land arrangement, preservation opportunity, or mission-aligned partnership.

Contact HOOD
How can I become a member?

Annual membership is $12. While payment setup is underway, you can register your interest and we will follow up when checkout is available. No payment is collected on the interest form.
