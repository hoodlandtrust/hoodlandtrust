Accessibility

HOOD is committed to making its website accessible to people with disabilities. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Using this website

The site includes keyboard navigation, a skip-to-content link, visible focus indicators, descriptive image text, labeled form controls, and status messages that assistive technology can announce. Pages adapt to smaller screens and browser zoom.

Need assistance or found a barrier?

Use the contact form and select “Accessibility assistance.” Tell us which page or task caused difficulty and your preferred way to communicate. Please share only the information needed to help you.

Accessibility is ongoing work. This statement is not a certification of legal compliance or a completed independent accessibility audit.
